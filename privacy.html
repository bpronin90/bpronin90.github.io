Kilo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Thank you for using Kilo.

Kilo is a workout and body-weight tracking application (the "App"), together with its website (the "Website") and related features and services (collectively, the "Services").

The owner(s) and operator(s) of the Services ("Kilo," "we," "us," or "our") are committed to respecting your privacy and protecting your personal information.

We use Kilo to keep track of our own workouts, just as you do. The App is local-first: you can record workouts and body weight without creating an account, and cloud synchronization is optional.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use Kilo, how it is used and protected, and the choices available to you. Please read it carefully. For questions or privacy concerns, email support@kilofitnesstracker.com.

Information Kilo handles

You can use Kilo locally without creating an account. Creating an account and enabling cloud sync are optional. If you choose these features, Kilo collects and stores the following information to provide account and synchronization functions:

  • your account email address and Kilo account user ID;
  • the profile name you choose to save (display_name);
  • body-weight entries, weight goals, and archived goals;
  • workout content, including workout notes and deload/fatigue history;
  • recovery records, including optional reasons you enter (which may reference injury or surgery), saved training-performance baselines, associated workout weeks, and recovery dates; and
  • selected preferences and feature toggles, including units, tracked lifts, workout-display state, and fatigue/deload settings.

Kilo can optionally store your date of birth, sex, height, and activity level on your device for fitness calculations. These fields are excluded from ordinary cloud synchronization but may be included in an export you create. Kilo does not request education, employment history, financial information, purchase history, or advertising and browsing profiles.

Device-local information and notifications

Your training history and app settings can be stored on your device. When you enable cloud sync, the selected preferences and feature toggles described above are also synced with your account. A stable per-install sync identifier stays on your device and is not uploaded. Local reminders are scheduled on your device only after you enable them. Kilo does not obtain, store, or transmit a push-notification token.

Diagnostics and service security

If a production build is configured with Sentry, Kilo sends crash and error diagnostics to Sentry to help identify and correct problems. Sentry diagnostics are not sent when that service is not configured. Kilo also uses Expo Updates to deliver application updates.

To protect account export, deletion, consent, and related services against abuse, Kilo temporarily retains limited rate-limit records associated with IP addresses, authenticated user IDs, or service identifiers. Different records have short retention windows, generally from one minute to two hours, with scheduled cleanup every 30 minutes.

Separately, Kilo records security events involving authentication, authorization, rate limiting, account exports and deletion, cloud-health-data deletion, restoration credentials, and service failures. These contain event categories, timestamps, outcomes, limited technical context, and where applicable pseudonymized digests derived from IP addresses or account IDs. They do not contain raw health entries. Security-event records become eligible for deletion after 90 days and are removed by a daily cleanup process.

How we use information

We use information to provide optional authentication and cloud sync, store and display training records and recovery information, provide reminders you request, support fitness estimates based on your inputs, protect account and data services, investigate abuse and security incidents, respond to support and privacy requests, handle account exports and deletion, diagnose faults when diagnostics are configured, and deliver app and web updates.

Service providers and optional social sign-in

We use Supabase for authentication, cloud storage, and related infrastructure. If you sign up or sign in using email and password, your credential is submitted to Supabase Auth; Kilo does not store plaintext passwords in its app data. You may optionally sign in using GitHub OAuth on web or Android. GitHub acts as an identity provider for that sign-in and does not host Kilo’s synced workout data.

Cloudflare Pages hosts Kilo’s web application. Cloudflare Turnstile supports bot and abuse prevention in supported account flows and processes connection, browser, and verification information, potentially including IP address, browser characteristics, and security signals. Cloudflare may process Turnstile data for its own bot-detection purposes as described in its applicable privacy terms.

If enabled, Sentry processes diagnostics. Expo Updates provides update delivery. Termly provides the external data-subject access and deletion request form linked below. We use these services to operate and protect Kilo, not to sell personal information or deliver targeted advertising.

Cloud health-data consent

Accounts and cloud synchronization are optional. In addition to enabling Cloud Sync, you must complete a separate affirmative consent step before Kilo stores your health and fitness records in its United States cloud database. Declining consent does not prevent you from using device-local features.

Account and settings: If you create an account or enable Cloud Sync, Kilo processes the email, account ID, optional display name, and eligible settings needed to provide those requested features.

Health and fitness information: The consent-gated cloud data includes body-weight entries and goals, workout notes, tracked lifts, deload/fatigue history, and recovery information (which may include an injury- or surgery-related reason you enter, performance baselines, related workout weeks, and recovery dates). The current in-app consent states: "I explicitly consent to Kilo storing the health and fitness data listed above in its United States cloud database for cross-device sync." Health data is not authorized for cloud storage solely by creating an account or enabling Cloud Sync. Kilo uses Supabase for this cloud storage.

Consent records: Kilo keeps a minimal pseudonymized record of consent choices for six years after account deletion to document the consent given or withdrawn. This record does not contain workout notes, measurements, or health entries.

Withdrawal: You may withdraw cloud-health-data consent in Cloud Sync settings. Once the server confirms withdrawal, ordinary cloud access to that health data is blocked and deletion of its cloud copy is queued. Deletion may remain pending and retry if services are unavailable. Your device-local training history remains unless separately deleted. Withdrawing consent does not reverse processing completed before withdrawal.

Exports and sharing you initiate

When you request a server account export, Kilo returns the export directly to you without retaining a server-side export artifact. Depending on the export type, an export may contain device-local profile information and records previously marked as deleted for synchronization. You may save or share exports using supported operating-system or browser controls. Copies you save or send outside Kilo are not deleted automatically when you delete your account.

Security and retention

Kilo uses HTTPS/TLS for network services. No transmission or storage mechanism is completely secure, but we use reasonable measures designed to protect information.

Cloud account and synced training data are retained while your account is active, subject to deletion, withdrawal, and limited security or legal retention described in this policy. Deleting an individual synced record can mark it as deleted while retaining record contents so deletion can propagate across devices. Such records may remain in synchronization storage and exported backup files. Withdrawing cloud-health-data consent or deleting your account uses a separate deletion process to remove the relevant cloud records, including records marked as deleted.

Rate-limit and security-event retention windows are described above. Minimal pseudonymized consent evidence is retained separately, as described under Cloud health-data consent. Copies in infrastructure backups may be subject to separate expiry schedules; the operational schedules are under review and are not represented here as immediate deletion guarantees.

Delete your Kilo account or data

You can delete your Kilo account in the app. Successful account deletion removes the account and its synced cloud data, except for limited records retained as described in this policy. By default, training history on the current device remains. You may instead choose an option to delete the account and wipe that device’s training history. Kilo separately reports if cloud account deletion succeeds but the local wipe does not complete. Other devices and previously exported files are not wiped by that device operation.

If you cannot access the app, request deletion through the Kilo data deletion request form or by emailing support@kilofitnesstracker.com. We may need to verify your identity. Limited records may be retained where necessary for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

Your privacy choices and requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have additional rights regarding your personal information. You can use Kilo's available tools or contact us to:

  • access personal information we hold about you;
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • request erasure of information;
  • receive a portable copy of information you provided in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format;
  • withdraw permission for cloud health-data processing using the Cloud Sync withdrawal control, as explained above;
  • raise concerns about how your information is handled; you may also contact a privacy or consumer-protection authority where applicable.

You can exercise rights using Kilo’s export and account-deletion features, the data deletion request form linked above, or by emailing support@kilofitnesstracker.com. Identity verification may be required before acting on some requests.

Children

Kilo is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under 13, do not create an account or submit personal information through the Services.

We encourage parents and guardians to supervise children's use of online services and explain the importance of protecting personal information. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information to Kilo, please email support@kilofitnesstracker.com so we can investigate and take appropriate steps, including deletion where required.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as Kilo evolves. The current policy and its update date will be posted on the policy page. Where required by applicable law, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent for material changes. For privacy questions or requests, contact support@kilofitnesstracker.com.
